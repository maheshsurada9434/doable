Privacy policy

Doable: To-Do & Daily Planner · Last updated 4 October 2026

In short: Doable does not collect, send or sell any of your data. Your tasks are stored only on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

What Doable stores

The tasks, lists, notes, subtasks and settings you create are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to a server run by the developer or anyone else.

What Doable collects

Nothing. Doable has no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no advertising SDKs and no tracking of any kind. The developer cannot see your tasks.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show reminders you set on tasks and the optional daily summary. You can turn this off at any time in Doable’s settings or in Android Settings.
  • Alarms and reminders – so reminders can arrive at the exact time you chose. Android may ask you to allow this.
  • Vibration – for the light tap when you complete or swipe a task. You can switch it off in Doable’s settings.
  • Run at startup – so your reminders are scheduled again after your phone restarts.

Backups

If you choose Export backup, Doable creates a file with your tasks and hands it to the app you pick (for example, Files or Google Drive). Where that file goes is up to you and is covered by that app’s privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete tasks inside the app, or uninstall Doable to remove everything it stored. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Doable does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
