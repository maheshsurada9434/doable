## App name (max 30)
Doable: To-Do & Daily Planner

## Short description (max 80)
Type tasks the way you say them. Get reminded on time. Private and offline.

## Full description (max 4000)
Doable is a simple, fast to-do list that understands plain English. Type “Pay rent tomorrow 9am #home !high” and Doable sets the date, time, tag and priority for you. No forms, no fiddling.

TYPE IT LIKE YOU’D SAY IT
• Dates: today, tomorrow, next friday, 15 oct, 31/12, in 3 days
• Times: 9am, 6:30pm, tonight, noon
• Repeats: every day, every weekday, every monday, monthly
• Tags with #, priority with !high / !med / !low, lists with @Work

NEVER MISS A THING
• Reminders at the time of a task, or 5 minutes to 1 day before
• A short morning summary of what’s planned for the day
• Repeating tasks schedule the next one automatically when you finish

PLAN YOUR DAY
• Today shows what’s due and what’s overdue, with a progress ring
• Upcoming groups the next two weeks by day
• Swipe right to complete, swipe left to move a task to tomorrow
• Drag to put tasks in the order you want
• Undo for every complete, move and delete

ORGANISE YOUR WAY
• Colour-coded lists for work, home, study or anything else
• Subtasks, notes, tags and three priority levels
• Search across titles, notes, subtasks and #tags
• A 25-minute focus timer for any task
• See your streak and how much you finished this week

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account and no sign-in
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Your tasks stay on your phone and work fully offline
• Export a backup file whenever you like

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Light and dark themes with six accent colours
• Gentle vibration and a soft chime when you finish a task (both optional)

Doable is free. Questions or ideas? Use “Send feedback” in Settings.
